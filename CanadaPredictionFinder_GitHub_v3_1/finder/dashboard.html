What happens after you press Start?

The scanner requests public, unauthenticated market data. The experimental strategy waits for 60 prior time-spaced samples, then looks for unusually large price moves and a possible reversion. It requires two scans of confirmation and skips expensive or illiquid candidates. It may take no trades.

Independent-value trading is separate: it only runs for exact contracts with a fresh forecast you supplied. There is no hidden AI subscription and no automatic expert forecast service.

Canada / British Columbia boundary

The built-in feed is Kalshi public research data, not a Canadian brokerage account. This release does not establish permission to trade directly on Kalshi from Canada. Some Canadian brokers offer eligible prediction contracts, but an exact matching contract, account permission and broker quote must be verified separately. This release has no ForecastEx live-quote adapter, no Wealthsimple login, and no order sender.

Your Mac is the host

Keep the application running and your Mac awake. Sleep, disconnection and source limits create gaps. The optional auto-start helper can restart the application after login. The 60-second target applies to the watchlist, not every prediction market.

Data is local

See the install guide for the persistent data directory. The database, logs and forecasts stay out of Git. Backups retain the seven newest local copies. A source outage pauses new opportunities; it never substitutes made-up prices.

Experimental results are not income

Fees and slippage are estimates. Depth can disappear and two observations do not guarantee real fills. The reversion strategy can mistake news for noise. Currency conversion, taxes, financing, incentive coupons and account-specific fees are not modeled.

Active configuration

Change the configuration file only with the app stopped. Strategy/risk changes after a first fill need a separate experiment directory; the ledger is never silently reset.
